    Questions

    About Friction Lab

    What is a workflow bottleneck?

    A bottleneck is the step whose limited capacity, processing time, errors, or handoffs constrain the performance of the larger system.

    Are the projected savings guaranteed?

    No. Friction Lab is an educational modeling tool. Its results show how the entered assumptions behave and help identify questions worth investigating in a real process.

    Does this tool upload business information?

    No. The simulation runs locally in your browser. A shared link contains only the selected scenario and daily volume unless you manually provide additional information elsewhere.

    Who built Friction Lab?

    Friction Lab was designed and developed by Alexis Gutowski, a software developer and systems problem solver based in Cape Coral, Florida.
